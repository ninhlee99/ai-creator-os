AICOS Creator Uploader

AICOS Creator Uploader is a personal, self-hosted tool used by a single creator to send videos they produced themselves to their own TikTok account as drafts. The creator then opens each draft in the TikTok app, reviews it, adds product links and music, and publishes it manually.

How it works

  1. The creator signs in with TikTok (Login Kit) and grants the user.info.basic and video.upload scopes.
  2. When a video is finished on the creator's own computer, the tool uploads it with the Content Posting API to the creator's TikTok inbox as a draft.
  3. Nothing is published automatically. The creator reviews and posts every video in the TikTok app.
