# Privacy Policy — AICOS Creator Uploader

Last updated: 2026-10-01

## What we access
- **Basic profile** (`user.info.basic`): your TikTok open ID and display name, used only to confirm which account is connected.
- **Video upload** (`video.upload`): permission to send videos you created to your TikTok inbox as drafts.

We do not access your followers, messages, analytics, other users' data, or any payment information.

## How data is stored
OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored only in a local file on the creator's own computer, readable only by that user. They are never sent to any server other than TikTok's official API, never logged, and never shared.

## Sharing
We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties. Videos are sent only to TikTok, at the creator's request.

## Retention and deletion
Tokens are kept until you disconnect. To delete all data: revoke access in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions) and delete the local token file. No other copy exists.

## Children
The tool is not intended for anyone under 18.
